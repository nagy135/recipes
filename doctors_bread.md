# Doctors Bread

## Ingredients

- 20g chia seeds
- 35g psyllium husk
- 150g rolled oats
- 150g mixed nuts, chopped
- 150g mixed seeds
- 1 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- 400ml water
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

## Instructions

- Line or grease a 2lb loaf tin
- Preheat oven to 190°C/170°C fan
- Mix chia, psyllium, oats, nuts, seeds, and salt
- Add water and olive oil, mix well
- Put mixture into tin and optionally soak for 30 minutes to 8 hours
- Bake 60 minutes
- Remove from tin and bake 15 minutes more
- Cool completely before slicing
