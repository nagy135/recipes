# Kaiserschmarrn

Austrian shredded pancake. Recipe scaled to 2 eggs; quantities rounded.

## Ingredients

- 267 ml milk
- 2 eggs
- 160 g semi-coarse wheat flour
- 2 tsp sugar
- 4 tsp raisins
- ⅔ tsp rum
- 33 g butter

## To cook and serve

- Oil for frying
- Cranberry compote or jam of your choice
- Pine nuts or walnuts (optional)
